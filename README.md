# Business Sales & Performance Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes a Superstore sales dataset from 2014–2017 to understand sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product categories, regions, and discounting.

The analysis was done using Excel and Tableau Public.

## Tools Used

- Excel
- Excel Pivot Tables
- Tableau Public

## Analysis

The project looks at:

- Monthly sales performance
- Sales by category
- Regional sales performance
- Sales by customer segment
- Sales vs. average discount
- Sales vs. profit

## Key Findings

- Sales increased significantly from 2015 to 2017.
- Technology generated the highest sales and had the largest overall increase among the three categories.
- West was the highest-performing region by sales.
- Consumer generated the highest sales among the customer segments.
- Average discount remained around 15–16% across the four years.
- Sales and profit did not always move in exactly the same way. In 2015, sales decreased while profit increased.

## Dashboard

The final Tableau dashboard brings the main findings together into a visual view of the business's sales and performance from 2014–2017.

## Dataset

Superstore dataset — sourced from Kaggle.
